About

I work at the intersection of business operations and data analytics — and I've been doing both at the same time long enough that they're genuinely inseparable.

The pattern that shows up consistently in my work: I see the full chain when most people only see their piece of it. Management can articulate that something is wrong. The data can show where it shows up. The people on the floor know why it actually happens. Those are three different things, and bridging them — translating between them, designing solutions that account for all of them — is the work I'm good at and the work I'm looking for.

I've spent four years in operational environments with real ownership. I write the documentation nobody thought to create. I pull the data, filter it to what actually matters, and interpret it against what I've watched happen in real life. I've been on the floor long enough to know why the numbers look the way they do — and that context is what makes the analysis actually useful rather than technically correct but operationally useless.

My technical projects demonstrate what I can build when given a real problem. Every project on this site was built with skills I didn't have at the start of the term it was assigned — not because I'm naturally talented, but because I learn what I need to learn, completely, for the purpose of applying it. My grades and my output reflect the same thing.

I want to sit between the business problem and the technical solution — not executing alone, but translating between the people who understand the problem and the systems that can solve it. The writing on this site is where those two worlds connect.
